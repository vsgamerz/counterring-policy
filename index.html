CounterRing Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game CounterRing (Android package com.vsgamerz.counterring) handles information. CounterRing is a free game made by VS Gamerz, an individual developer. It is not made by a company. In this policy, "we" and "the developer" mean VS Gamerz.

Summary

  • The game has no accounts and does not ask you to sign in.
  • The developer does not collect, store, or sell your personal information.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect some data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Game progress

Your cleared levels, stars, hints, glaze beads, kiln progress, the unlocked board glaze, and settings (sound, vibration, dark mode, and Reduce motion) are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. They are never sent to the developer. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this progress is deleted.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

CounterRing uses Google AdMob to show rewarded video ads. These ads appear only when you choose to watch a video to get a hint. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language, and IP address (which can indicate your approximate location),
  • usage data about ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and whether they were watched.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are used. You can choose whether to allow personalised ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Learn more in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

CounterRing may use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played (for example, which levels are cleared and how hints are used) and to fix crashes. This can include app events, device type, operating system version, an app instance identifier, and crash logs. This data does not include your name, email, or contacts, and it is not used by the developer to identify you. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Permissions

The game uses internet and network state access to load ads, vibration for optional haptic feedback, and the advertising ID permission used by Google's ads SDK. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location.

Children

CounterRing is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing and selling

The developer does not sell or share your personal information. Data collected by Google through AdMob or Firebase is handled under Google's own policies linked above.

Your choices

  • Change your ad consent choice, reset your advertising ID, or limit ad personalisation in your device settings.
  • Delete all game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
